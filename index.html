Routeless

Rural Student Transportation Recovery System

01 / 04 Student Portal Drag or swipe

Routeless coordinates student transportation demand with local drivers and school transport options during rural disruptions. Demand thresholds route lower-volume requests to local drivers and escalate high-demand emergencies to school operations. Parents can track transit status, while ExamBridge helps students facing urgent exam-related disruptions consider alternative exam-centre arrangements.

Highlights

  • Role-based portals for Students, School Operations, Local Drivers, and Parents
  • Student reports for missing buses and transportation breakdowns
  • Demand thresholds coordinate local-driver routing and high-demand school response
  • Parent transit-status tracking and updates
  • ExamBridge suggestions for alternative exam-centre arrangements

Built with

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
